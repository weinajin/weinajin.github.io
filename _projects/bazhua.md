I founded the [Bazhua.org](https://bazhua.org/) website in 2011. Together with 26 of my colleagues who were attending physicians or medical residents, we wrote articles to the public on medicine.

In 2013, our articles in [Bazhua.org](https://bazhua.org/) were published as a book, 'The Health Topics that Doctors Want to Discuss with You' (《给时光以生命: 医生想跟你聊的健康微话题》).
