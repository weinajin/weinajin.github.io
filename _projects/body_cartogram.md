Pain is a universal human experience. It appears in different part of the human body with various incidences.

I designed this visualization maps to intuitively show the pain prevalence data on different body areas.

The corresponding area of the body will enlarge when that body part is more affected by pain.

Users can interact with the slider to inspect the pain data change over time.
