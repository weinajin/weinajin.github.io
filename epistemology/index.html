Feminist Epistemology and Medical Imaging

I am slowly working on a reading project that explores the intersection of feminist epistemology and medical imaging.

Here is the reading list that is recommended by the Prof. Susan Erikson. Thank you Prof. Erikson!

  1. The Visible Woman: Imaging Technologies, Gender, and Science
  2. Looking Within: A Sociocultural Examination of Fetoscopy. By Deborah Blizzard
  3. Baby’s First Picture: Ultrasound and the Politics of Fetal Subjects. By LISA M. MITCHELL
  4. Fetal Positions: Individualism, Science, Visuality. By Karen Newman
  5. Modest_Witness@Second_Millennium. FemaleMan_Meets_OncoMouse: Feminism and Technoscience. Donna J. Haraway, Lynn M. Randolph (Illustrator)
  6. The Woman beneath the Skin: A Doctor’s Patients in Eighteenth-Century Germany. By Barbara Duden. Translated by Thomas Dunlap
  7. Feminist Science Studies: A New Generation. Edited By Maralee Mayberry, Banu Subramaniam, Lisa Weasel
  8. Latour, Science in Action (1987)
  9. The Visual Culture Reader. By: Mirzoeff, Nicholas
  10. About Looking. By John Berger
  11. Wild Science: Reading Feminism, Medicine and the Media. Edited By Janine Marchessault, Kim Sawchuk
  12. Living and Working with the New Medical Technologies: Intersections of Inquiry. Edited by Margaret Lock, Allan Young, Alberto Cambrosio
  13. Testing Women, Testing the Fetus: The Social Impact of Amniocentesis in America. By Rayna Rapp
  14. When the Moon Waxes Red: Representation, Gender and Cultural Politics. By Trinh T. Minh-ha
  15. Pregnant Pictures. By Sandra Matthews, Laura Wexler
  16. The Educated Eye: Visual Culture and Pedagogy in the Life Sciences
  17. Magnetic Appeal: MRI and the Myth of Transparency. By Kelly Joyce
  18. The Making of the Unborn Patient: A Social Anatomy of Fetal Surgery. By Monica J. Casper
  19. Icons of Life: A Cultural History of Human Embryos
